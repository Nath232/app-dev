###Tittle: All of Us are dead
Genre: Horror, Thriller, Survival, Drama, and Action
Introduction:All of Us Are Dead is a South Korean television series about a zombie outbreak that begins in a high school. The story follows a group of students who become trapped inside their school while trying to survive against infected people. They must work together, make difficult decisions, and protect one another as the situation becomes more dangerous. Throughout the series, the students experience fear, friendship, love, sacrifice, and betrayal. It shows how people react and make choices when their lives are in danger.
Synopsis
All of Us Are Dead begins when a science experiment at Hyosan High School goes wrong, causing a dangerous zombie virus to spread throughout the school. Students suddenly find themselves surrounded by infected classmates and teachers who attack anyone they see. Nam On-jo, Lee Cheong-san, Choi Nam-ra, Lee Su-hyeok, and their friends try to find a safe way to escape. Along the way, they face many challenges, including hunger, fear, betrayal, and the loss of people they care about. Some students show bravery and teamwork, while others make selfish decisions just to save themselves. As the outbreak spreads throughout the city, the survivors realize that they cannot depend on anyone but themselves. Their journey becomes a fight for survival, friendship, and hope.
